# DHANTERAS: THE OCEAN THAT GAVE US THE GIFT OF LIFE

**A Mythological Storytelling Experience for Children | Ages 6–10**

## EPISODE 1 — THE OCEAN THAT HELD A SECRET

**NARRATOR:**

Children, imagine that tonight, when the world becomes quiet and the stars begin to twinkle, you discover a secret.

Far, far away, beyond the mountains, beyond the forests, beyond even the clouds, lies a magnificent ocean.

But this is no ordinary ocean.

It is the Ocean of Milk, known as *Kshira Sagara* — a magical ocean whose depths hold extraordinary treasures.

Hidden beneath its beautiful waves are precious jewels, wonderful creatures, divine gifts, and something more precious than anything else.

Something the devas and asuras both desperately want.

It is called **amrit**, the divine nectar associated with immortality.

Long ago, the devas, who protected the heavenly worlds, had lost their strength and splendour. They needed help to regain their power.

So, they approached Lord Vishnu.

Lord Vishnu listened to their troubles and revealed a plan.

“The nectar you seek lies hidden within the great Ocean of Milk. To obtain it, the ocean must be churned.”

But there was one enormous problem.

The ocean was too vast for the devas to churn alone.

They would need help — even from their rivals, the asuras.

And so, the devas and asuras agreed to work together for a common goal.

Together, they would attempt something no one had ever done before.

They would churn the entire cosmic ocean!

**NARRATOR — INVITING THE CHILDREN:**

And guess what, children?

Today, you are not just listening to this story.

You are our special **Ocean Explorers!**

But before our adventure can begin, the sleeping ocean needs to wake up.

Are you ready to help?

**INTERACTIVE MOMENT — WAKE UP THE OCEAN**

**NARRATOR:**

First, let us wake up the waves!

Move your hands gently from side to side, just like the ocean.

Wonderful!

Now, can you hear the ocean creatures sleeping beneath the water?

Let us call them with a gentle sound.

“Blub, blub, blub!”

And finally, let us awaken the ocean's magic. Clap your hands three times and say:

**ALL:** “Ocean of Wonders, wake up!”

**NARRATOR:**

Look! The waves are moving. The fish are dancing. The lotus flowers are opening.

The Ocean of Wonders is awake!

But our great adventure is only beginning.

---

## EPISODE 2 — THE MOUNTAIN, THE SERPENT, AND THE GREAT PLAN

**NARRATOR:**

Lord Vishnu now explained what they would need.

“To churn an ocean this vast,” He said, “we will need a strong mountain, a mighty rope, and a firm foundation.”

First came **Mount Mandara**, a magnificent mountain that would act as the churning stick.

Next came **Vasuki**, the great serpent king, who would serve as the rope.

The devas would pull from one side.

The asuras would pull from the other.

By pulling first one way and then the other, they would turn the mountain and churn the ocean.

But tell me, children, have you ever tried to move something very heavy all by yourself?

Perhaps a big box, a table, or a pile of toys?

Sometimes, when something is too difficult for one person, working together makes it possible.

That was exactly what the devas and asuras needed to do.

They gathered around the great mountain.

Vasuki curled his enormous body around Mount Mandara, forming a living rope.

The devas took one side.

The asuras took the other.

Everyone was ready.

Almost everyone, that is.

There was just one tiny problem.

Mount Mandara was enormously heavy!

As the churning began, the mountain started sinking into the ocean.

Down it went.

Lower.

Lower.

Lower!

“Oh no!” cried the devas. “Our mountain is sinking! How will we churn the ocean now?”

The asuras watched in alarm.

Their great plan was about to fail before it had even begun.

Just when everyone thought there was no way forward, Lord Vishnu came to their rescue.

He took the form of **Kurma, the divine tortoise**.

Kurma dived beneath the ocean and supported Mount Mandara on His mighty back.

The mountain was steady once more.

The great churning could begin!

**INTERACTIVE MOMENT — HELP KURMA HOLD THE MOUNTAIN**

**NARRATOR:**

Oh, Ocean Explorers! The mountain is sinking!

Can you help us?

Slowly crouch down as the mountain sinks.

Lower... lower... lower!

But wait!

Someone is coming to help!

Kurma has arrived!

Bring your hands together like a tortoise shell.

Now, let us help Kurma lift the mountain!

Slowly stand up and stretch your hands towards the sky.

Higher... higher... and hold!

**NARRATOR:**

Amazing! The mountain is steady.

Kurma is supporting Mount Mandara, and the great churning can finally begin.

Devas on one side!

Asuras on the other!

Hold the rope tightly.

The Ocean of Milk is waiting to reveal its treasures.

---

## EPISODE 3 — LET THE GREAT CHURNING BEGIN!

**NARRATOR:**

The moment had arrived.

The devas pulled.

The asuras pulled.

Vasuki stretched around Mount Mandara.

The mountain began to turn.

Round and round it went!

The ocean stirred.

The waves grew taller.

Bubbles rose to the surface.

Fish darted between the swirling waves, and the whole universe seemed to tremble with excitement.

But the nectar did not appear.

The devas pulled harder.

The asuras pulled harder.

Still, nothing.

“What should we do?” wondered the devas.

“Should we give up?” asked the asuras.

But they had come too far to stop now.

So they tried again.

And again.

And again!

**NARRATOR — ENCOURAGING THE CHILDREN:**

Ocean Explorers, the churning needs your energy!

Can you help the devas and asuras?

**INTERACTIVE MOMENT — THE CHURNING CHALLENGE**

**NARRATOR:**

Everyone, hold your imaginary rope!

Pull! Pull! Pull!

Now, let us turn the great mountain.

Churn! Churn! Churn!

Once more, with all your energy!

Pull! Pull! Pull!

Churn! Churn! Churn!

And now, everyone together, call out:

**ALL:** “Ocean, reveal your magic!”

**NARRATOR:**

Look at the ocean!

Something is happening beneath the waves.

The water is glowing.

Tiny golden bubbles are rising to the surface.

Something is coming!

But wait...

The first thing to emerge is not a treasure.

It is something dangerous.

---

## EPISODE 4 — THE TERRIBLE POISON

**NARRATOR:**

As the ocean churned, a terrible poison called **Halahala** emerged from its depths.

It was so powerful that it threatened the whole universe.

The devas were frightened.

The asuras stepped back.

“What have we brought into the world?” they cried.

The poison spread, and all creation was in danger.

The churning stopped.

Everyone looked around, hoping someone would know what to do.

Then, Lord Shiva stepped forward.

He understood that if nobody acted, countless living beings could suffer.

With great compassion, Shiva took in the terrible poison to protect creation and held it in His throat.

The poison turned His throat blue.

From that moment, He became known as **Neelkanth, the blue-throated Lord**.

The universe was saved.

The devas and asuras watched in wonder.

They had begun this adventure in search of a treasure for themselves.

But now, they had witnessed something far greater.

Lord Shiva had put the welfare of the universe before His own comfort.

They had learned that true strength was not just about winning or becoming powerful.

Sometimes, the greatest act of strength is protecting others.

**NARRATOR — GENTLY INVITING THE CHILDREN:**

Children, let us take a little pause.

Place one hand on your heart.

Take a gentle breath in.

And slowly breathe out.

Now, imagine sending a little light of kindness to everyone around you.

To your family.

To your friends.

To animals, birds, and every living creature.

Let us make three wishes together.

**NARRATOR:** May everyone be safe.

**CHILDREN:** May everyone be safe.

**NARRATOR:** May everyone be healthy.

**CHILDREN:** May everyone be healthy.

**NARRATOR:** May everyone be happy.

**CHILDREN:** May everyone be happy.

**NARRATOR:**

Beautiful!

The dark clouds began to fade.

The ocean slowly returned to its beautiful shades of blue and gold.

Lord Shiva's compassion had reminded everyone that the welfare of all creation mattered.

And now, the great churning could continue.

What would emerge next?

A jewel?

A magical animal?

Or the treasure they had all been waiting for?

---

## EPISODE 5 — THE OCEAN REVEALS ITS TREASURES

**NARRATOR:**

The devas and asuras returned to their places.

They held Vasuki once more.

Mount Mandara turned.

The ocean swirled.

And slowly, one extraordinary wonder after another began to appear.

First came **Kamadhenu**, the divine wish-fulfilling cow, associated with abundance and generosity.

Then came a magnificent celestial horse, shining with extraordinary beauty.

Next, a majestic white elephant named **Airavata** emerged from the ocean.

Its magnificent form gleamed against the waves.

Precious jewels glimmered in the sunlight.

A divine tree, known as **Kalpavriksha**, appeared, its branches filled with heavenly blossoms.

And then, amid the ocean's shining waves, **Goddess Lakshmi** appeared in divine splendour.

Her presence brought thoughts of prosperity, fortune, and abundance.

The devas watched in amazement.

The asuras looked upon the extraordinary treasures.

The ocean was full of wonders!

One beautiful gift after another had emerged from its depths.

But the great churning was not yet finished.

There was still one very special gift waiting to appear.

A gift that would remind the world why health is one of life's greatest treasures.

**INTERACTIVE MOMENT — REVEAL THE OCEAN'S HIDDEN TREASURES**

**NARRATOR:**

Ocean Explorers, look carefully!

The ocean has hidden some of its wonderful treasures behind magical lotus flowers.

Can you help us reveal them?

First, let us welcome Kamadhenu!

Make a gentle cow sound and pretend to offer her a flower.

Wonderful!

Now, let us welcome Airavata!

Raise one arm and move it like an elephant's trunk.

Splendid!

Next, let us help Kalpavriksha grow!

Stretch your arms upwards like a tiny seed growing into a mighty tree.

Higher and higher!

And finally, let us welcome Goddess Lakshmi.

Bring your hands together and gently open them into the shape of a lotus flower.

**NARRATOR:**

Look at all the treasures you have helped reveal!

The divine cow, the celestial elephant, the wish-fulfilling tree, and Goddess Lakshmi.

The ocean has shared so many wonders.

But something is still missing.

The waves are glowing brighter.

A golden light is appearing at the centre of the ocean.

One final lotus remains closed.

Who could be coming?

Let us watch carefully.

---

## EPISODE 6 — THE DIVINE ARRIVAL OF LORD DHANVANTARI

**NARRATOR — SLOWLY BUILDING EXCITEMENT:**

The ocean grew still.

The waves shimmered.

A beautiful golden light spread across the water.

The devas stopped pulling.

The asuras stopped watching the rope.

Everyone turned towards the glowing ocean.

And then...

From the depths of the Ocean of Milk emerged a radiant divine being.

His face was calm.

His presence was gentle.

His form shone with divine splendour.

He carried a beautiful pot that gleamed like gold.

It was **Lord Dhanvantari**, revered in Hindu tradition as the divine physician and associated with the knowledge of Ayurveda and healing.

In His hands was the precious pot of amrit.

The devas gazed at Him in wonder.

The asuras stared at the shining pot.

After all their effort, the nectar they had sought had finally appeared.

But this moment held a lesson even more precious than the glittering pot.

Lord Dhanvantari's appearance reminds us of the value of health, healing, and caring for life.

For what good are beautiful treasures if we cannot enjoy the world around us?

What good is wealth if we forget to care for ourselves and those we love?

Gold and jewels may bring comfort and joy.

But our health helps us learn, play, explore, laugh, and share precious moments with the people around us.

Health is a treasure worth protecting.

And caring for life is one of the most meaningful ways to use the gifts we have been given.

This is one of the beautiful meanings we can carry into Dhanteras, a festival traditionally associated with Lord Dhanvantari and the celebration of health and prosperity.

**INTERACTIVE MOMENT — HELP THE DIVINE LIGHT APPEAR**

**NARRATOR:**

Ocean Explorers, the final lotus is still closed.

Lord Dhanvantari is waiting to appear in all His divine splendour.

Let us help the light awaken!

First, rub your palms together gently to create a little warmth.

Now, slowly open your hands.

Stretch them towards the centre of the ocean, as if you are sharing your own little light.

Are you ready?

Let us call out together:

**ALL:** “Lord Dhanvantari, bring the light of health!”

Once more, with joy!

**ALL:** “Lord Dhanvantari, bring the light of health!”

**NARRATOR:**

Look!

The lotus is opening.

The golden light is growing brighter.

The ocean is glowing with beautiful patterns of gold, white, and blue.

And there He is!

Lord Dhanvantari has appeared, holding the precious pot of amrit.

Well done, Ocean Explorers!

You helped our story reach its most wonderful moment.

The Ocean of Wonders has given us a precious lesson.

Wealth is wonderful.

Beautiful things bring joy.

But health, kindness, and the ability to care for one another are treasures we should never take for granted.

And that, dear Ocean Explorers, is one of the beautiful meanings we can carry with us into Dhanteras.

## EPISODE 7 — WHAT IS TRUE WEALTH?

**NARRATOR:**

Children, before we say goodbye to the Ocean of Wonders, let us imagine something.

Suppose Lord Dhanvantari has offered you a magical treasure pot.

It is beautiful and golden, with tiny lotus flowers carved all around it.

But this is no ordinary pot.

You can fill it with the things you believe make life truly precious.

What would you place inside?

Would you choose good health, so you can run, play, learn, and grow?

Would you choose love and family, so you always have people to care for and share your happiness with?

Would you choose kindness, so you can help someone who needs you?

Would you choose wisdom, so you can learn to make thoughtful choices?

Or would you choose gold and jewels, beautiful treasures that can bring comfort and joy?

There is room in your magical pot for many wonderful things.

Let us discover what matters most to you.

**INTERACTIVE ACTIVITY — FILL THE TREASURE POT**

**NARRATOR:**

Look at the treasures before you, little Ocean Explorers.

Choose the things you would place inside your magical pot.

**GOOD HEALTH**

Taking care of our bodies and minds.

**LOVE AND FAMILY**

Caring for the people around us.

**KINDNESS**

Helping others and showing compassion.

**WISDOM**

Learning how to live well and make good choices.

**GOLD AND JEWELS**

Material treasures that can bring comfort and joy.

**NARRATOR — AFTER THE CHILDREN MAKE THEIR CHOICES:**

Look at the treasures you have chosen!

Every one of these can have a place in a meaningful life.

Gold and jewels can be beautiful.

Prosperity can help us care for ourselves and others.

But we must also remember to protect our health, share our love, act with kindness, and keep learning.

The greatest treasure is not always the one that shines the brightest.

Sometimes, it is the one that helps us live well and care for the people around us.

---

## THE CLOSING — A LITTLE LIGHT FOR DHANTERAS

**NARRATOR:**

And now, dear Ocean Explorers, our great adventure has come to an end.

Today, we travelled to the magnificent Ocean of Milk.

We helped awaken its waves.

We helped Kurma support the mighty mountain.

We pulled the great serpent rope and churned the ocean together.

We witnessed Lord Shiva protect the universe from the terrible poison.

We discovered the extraordinary treasures hidden beneath the waves.

And finally, we welcomed Lord Dhanvantari, the divine physician, carrying the precious pot of amrit.

But the most important thing we discovered was not simply a treasure hidden beneath the ocean.

It was a lesson we can carry in our hearts.

True prosperity is not only about what we own.

It is also about how we live, how we care for ourselves, and how we treat others.

So, this Dhanteras, when you see a little diya glowing in your home, remember the great Ocean of Milk.

Remember the devas and asuras who worked together.

Remember Lord Shiva, who protected creation.

And remember Lord Dhanvantari, who appeared carrying the precious pot of amrit.

May your homes be filled with light.

May your families be blessed with health and happiness.

May your hearts be filled with kindness.

And may you always remember that one of life's greatest treasures is the gift of being able to care for one another.

**Happy Dhanteras, little Ocean Explorers!**
