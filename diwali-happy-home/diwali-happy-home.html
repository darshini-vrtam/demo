शङ्खचक्रगदाहस्ते महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

Namaste'stu Mahamaye, Shripithe Surapujite,Shankha Chakra Gada Haste, Mahalakshmi Namostute.

Meaning

“O Goddess Mahalakshmi, the great Divine Goddess, worshipped by the Devas, who holds the conch, discus and mace in Your hands — I bow to You with respect.”
