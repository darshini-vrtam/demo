## Episode 1

### Scene 1
“Long ago, in the beautiful kingdom of Ayodhya, lived Prince Rama, his wife Sita, and his devoted brother Lakshmana.
Rama was known for his courage, kindness and truthfulness. But one day, his life took an unexpected turn.
Rama was asked to leave the palace and spend fourteen years in the forest.
Though the decision was difficult, Rama accepted it calmly. Sita chose to accompany him, and Lakshmana joined them too.
And so, their extraordinary journey began.”

### Scene 2
“The forest was filled with tall trees, flowing streams and the songs of birds. Rama, Sita and Lakshmana made a peaceful home among nature.
But far away, in the kingdom of Lanka, lived Ravana, a powerful king.
One day, Ravana took Sita away to Lanka.
When Rama discovered that Sita was missing, he was heartbroken. But he did not give up.
With Lakshmana beside him, Rama began searching for her.”

### Scene 3
“Oh, young explorers! Can you help Rama and Lakshmana discover which way Sita went?
Look carefully. Which clues can help us follow the path?”
“You found the clues! Every little discovery brings Rama one step closer to finding Sita. Let’s keep going!”

### Scene 4
“During his search, Rama met Hanuman, a brave and devoted helper.
Hanuman promised to help find Sita. With courage in his heart, he leapt across the mighty ocean towards Lanka.
There, he discovered Sita sitting beneath a tree in the Ashoka grove.
Hanuman approached her gently and gave her Rama’s ring.
‘Sita, Rama has not forgotten you,’ he reassured her. ‘He is coming to bring you home.’
For the first time in a long while, Sita felt hope.”

### Scene 5
“Can you help Hanuman deliver Rama’s ring to Sita? Guide him along the path and make sure the precious ring reaches her safely!”
“Well done! A small ring carried a very big message: Rama was coming, and hope was alive.”

### Scene 6
“Hanuman returned with news of Sita. Rama now knew where she was.
But there was one enormous challenge. How could the army cross the ocean and reach Lanka?
The vanaras came together with a plan. They would build a bridge using stones and rocks.
Every helper had a part to play. Some carried stones, some moved branches, and others helped arrange the path.
One by one, the pieces came together.”

### Scene 7
“The bridge is almost ready! Can you help the vanaras place the stones in the empty spaces?”
“Look at what you have helped create! Every stone mattered, and every helper made a difference.”

### Scene 8
“Across the bridge marched Rama, Lakshmana, Hanuman and their brave companions.
At last, they reached Lanka.
Beyond its grand gates, Sita waited. Ahead lay a great challenge, but Rama was not alone. His friends stood beside him, united by courage, loyalty and hope.
Would they be able to defeat Ravana and bring Sita home?
The gates of Lanka opened.
The final part of their journey was about to begin…”

## Episode 2

### Scene 1
“Rama and his companions had reached Lanka, but Ravana would not let Sita go easily.
A great battle began. The sounds of bows and clashing weapons echoed through the kingdom.
Rama stood firm. He remembered why he had come: not for fame or anger, but to rescue Sita and restore what was right.
At last, Rama faced Ravana with courage and determination. Rama’s final arrow struck its mark, and Ravana was defeated.
The battle was over.
The story reminds us that power alone does not make someone right. Courage, responsibility and the choice to do what is right matter far more.”

### Scene 2
“After the battle, Rama and Sita were reunited.
Hanuman and their companions rejoiced. Their long journey had been filled with hardship, but their courage and friendship had carried them through.
Now there was one more journey to make.
They would return to Ayodhya, where their story had begun.”

### Scene 3
“Rama, Sita and Lakshmana began their journey home.
From above, they saw rivers winding through the land, forests stretching to the horizon and villages surrounded by fields.
At last, Ayodhya appeared in the distance.
The people had waited a long time for Rama’s return. Soon, the city would be filled with joy.”

### Scene 4
“When the people of Ayodhya heard that Rama was returning, excitement spread through the city.
Families decorated their homes with flowers. Children made colourful rangoli patterns. Sweet treats were prepared, and little clay lamps were placed along doorways, windows and rooftops.
As the sun began to set, the city waited in joyful anticipation.
Soon, the first lamps would shine.”

### Scene 5
“Rama is coming home! But Ayodhya is still waiting for its lights.
Can you help the people welcome him? Tap the diyas to light them one by one!”
“Beautiful! Look how the light spreads from one little lamp to the next. Together, the people of Ayodhya have made the whole city shine!”

### Scene 6
“At last, Rama, Sita and Lakshmana returned to Ayodhya.
The people welcomed them with joy. The streets shone with rows of little lamps, and the air was filled with celebration.
The lamps were more than decorations. They showed the people’s happiness, hope and love.
This joyful homecoming is one of the stories remembered during Diwali, the festival of lights.
Even a small flame can brighten a dark place. And even a small act of kindness can brighten someone’s day.”

### Scene 7
“Have you ever looked closely at a diya?
Its flame is small, yet it shines through the darkness.
The diya reminds us to bring light into the world through our actions: by speaking kindly, helping someone who needs us, being brave when something is difficult, and sharing what we have.
We may not be able to light every lamp in the world, but we can each bring a little light to someone.”

### Scene 8
“Many families say a beautiful shloka when they light a lamp. It reminds us of the goodness and well-being that light represents.
Let’s listen to the words.”

दीप श्लोक
शुभं करोति कल्याणम्
आरोग्यं धनसम्पदा ।
शत्रुबुद्धिविनाशाय
दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तुते ॥

Śubhaṃ karoti kalyāṇam
Ārogyaṃ dhana-sampadā
Śatru-buddhi-vināśāya
Dīpa-jyotir namo'stu te

“May the light bring goodness and well-being, health and prosperity. May harmful thoughts be overcome. I offer my respect to the sacred light of the lamp.”

“The shloka teaches us to welcome goodness and well-being into our lives.
As you see a diya glowing, remember: let your thoughts be kind, let your actions help others, and let your courage shine when someone needs you.
Now, shall we say the shloka together once more?”

### Scene 9
“Now it’s your turn to spread a little light!
Choose one kind thing you can do. Will you help someone, share with a friend, comfort someone who feels sad, or care for nature?”
“Your diya is beautiful! And your kind action can make it even brighter. Remember, the light we share through kindness can last long after a lamp goes out.”

### Scene 10
“And so, the city of Ayodhya shone with the light of countless diyas.
The story of Rama’s journey reminds us that courage can help us through difficult times, friendship makes us stronger, and hope can guide us forward.
Every Diwali, families celebrate with lamps, prayers, sweets and time together. The glowing diya reminds us of the light we can bring into our homes and into the lives of others.
So this Diwali, when you see a little lamp shining, remember your own little light.
Be brave. Be kind. Help others. And whenever you can, make someone’s world a little brighter.
Happy Diwali! May your home be filled with joy, your heart with kindness, and your life with light.”
